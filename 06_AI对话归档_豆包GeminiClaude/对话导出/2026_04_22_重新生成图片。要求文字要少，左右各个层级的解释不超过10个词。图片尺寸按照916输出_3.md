Key distinction in different fields:
In Computer Science & Statistics (Normalization vs. Standardization):

Normalization typically means scaling numerical data to fall within a specific range, often 0 to 1. It makes different datasets comparable without skewing them by their absolute values.
Standardization usually refers to shifting the distribution of data so that it has a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one (Standard Normal Distribution).

In Sociology or Behavior:

Normalization is the process through which ideas and behaviors that might be considered "outside the norm" become regarded as "normal" over time.
